Nebula Reset Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Nebula Reset is a single-player game. This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle. It applies to every version of the game: the Google Play version and the versions downloaded from GitHub or itch.io.

What the game itself collects

The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. To find out which devices it runs slowly on and whether it is too easy or too hard, every version of the game (Google Play, GitHub and itch.io) sends anonymous statistics to Google Analytics, unless you turn them off:

  • when the game starts: the game's version, your device's model, operating system and version, graphics chip, screen size and refresh rate, the graphics renderer and your device's language setting;
  • when a level ends: which level, character and ascension it was, whether you won, lost or left it, the HP it cost, how long it took and how smoothly it ran (frame times, slow-frame time, how long drawing took);
  • when a run ends: how far it got, how it ended, the score, the HP each boss fight cost, the upgrades you picked and how long you played.

These statistics are tied to a random number made when the game first sends them, kept only in the game's settings file. They contain no name, email, account, advertising ID or contacts, and the game does not use them for ads. Google Analytics receives your IP address with each request, as any website does, and may derive an approximate region from it. The data is kept for 2 months and is used only to improve the game. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can turn the statistics off at any time in Options (Send anonymous statistics). In the Google Play version they are not sent until you have answered Google's consent form where the law requires one. Statistics waiting to be sent are deleted when you turn them off, and uninstalling the game deletes the random number.

The game also keeps a few files on your device so you can pick up where you left off: your high scores, which characters and ascension levels you have unlocked, your character mastery, the run in progress, your run history and replays, and (Google Play version only) whether you have bought "Remove ads". These files never leave your device unless your device's own backup copies them or you export a replay. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads (Google Play version only)

The Google Play version shows a small banner ad served by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choice wherever the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). AdMob and Google's consent service may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and diagnostic data.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise them. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can change your consent at any time from Privacy choices in the game's main menu, where it applies to you. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings, Google, Ads). Buying "Remove ads" removes the banner and stops ad requests.

The versions from GitHub and itch.io contain no ads and no ad services.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game only receives confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account and keeps that on your device. The developer never sees your payment details. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

Children

Nebula Reset is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them. The anonymous statistics described above are the same for every player and contain nothing that identifies a child. The ads served are not personalised for users who have not consented.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
