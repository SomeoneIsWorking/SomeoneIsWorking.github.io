Devoid Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Devoid is a single-player game. This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle. It applies to every version of the game: the Google Play version and the version downloaded from GitHub.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own, and it sends nothing about you or your play to the developer.

It keeps a few files on your device: your high score, whether the background stars and the music are on, and (Google Play version only) whether you have bought "Remove ads". These files never leave your device unless your device's own backup copies them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads (Google Play version only)

The Google Play version shows a small banner ad served by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choice wherever the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). AdMob and Google's consent service may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and diagnostic data.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise them. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can change your consent at any time from Privacy choices in the game's pause menu, where it applies to you. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings, Google, Ads). Buying "Remove ads" removes the banner and stops ad requests.

The version from GitHub contains no ads and no ad services.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game only receives confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account and keeps that on your device. The developer never sees your payment details. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

Children

Devoid is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them. The ads served are not personalised for users who have not consented.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play store page.
